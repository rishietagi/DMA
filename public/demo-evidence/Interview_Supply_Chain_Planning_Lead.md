# Interview notes — Supply Chain Planning Lead — Illustrative

> Illustrative notes created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Stakeholder role only; not a real interview.

Date: 24 Sep 2026. Interviewer: KPMG.

IBP gives us one agreed plan across sales, supply and finance — a single source of truth for the monthly cycle.

Under-forecasting on quick commerce is our biggest fill-rate problem.

Transport is planned by phone with transporters; we have no TMS.

Master data errors such as duplicate materials still break the forecast hierarchy.

The supply chain dashboard is integrated with SAP and refreshed daily.
