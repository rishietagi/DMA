# Interview transcript — Warehouse & Logistics Manager — Illustrative

> Illustrative transcript created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Stakeholder role only; not a real interview.

Date: 28 Sep 2026. Interviewer: KPMG. Site: Haridwar depot.

KPMG: Walk us through how stock moves through the depot today.

Manager: Goods receipts are posted in SAP when the truck arrives, so inbound is integrated with SAP.

Manager: Cycle counts are still done on paper and keyed into Excel at the end of the shift.

KPMG: How do you know where a pallet sits in the warehouse?

Manager: There is no WMS, so bin locations live in a manual register at each depot.

KPMG: And outbound — how are loads planned?

Manager: Transport loads are planned by phone and Excel with each transporter every morning.

Manager: Proof of delivery comes back as paper slips, so freight bills are reconciled manually.

KPMG: What works well?

Manager: Fill rate and OTIF are on the supply chain dashboard, integrated with SAP and refreshed daily.

Manager: Energy and water use for the sustainability report are collected manually in Excel once a month.
