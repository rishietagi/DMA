# Digital & IT strategy FY27 — Illustrative

> Illustrative document created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Not a Himalaya document.

## Context

The FY26 self-assessment showed that the gap is not technology investment; it is scaled adoption.

## Priorities

Demand forecasting is the top data priority: under-forecasting continues to hurt fill rate on quick commerce and modern trade.

Order induction for marketplaces is yet to be integrated; the FY27 target is one connected layer for all platforms.

A GenAI pilot for PDP content is under way with the agency.

Master data quality is a known blocker; duplicate vendor records slow down onboarding.

Marketing spend is captured at brand level only, so ROI at PG level cannot be measured.

## Ways of working

There is no forum yet to approve or monitor AI use cases across functions.
