# E-commerce order-to-cash SOP v2.1 — Illustrative

> Illustrative document created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Not a Himalaya document.

## 1. Order induction

Purchase orders from Amazon, Flipkart and quick-commerce platforms are downloaded from each seller portal every morning.

The order desk keys each PO into SAP by hand and checks prices against the price master in Excel.

## 2. Appointments and dispatch

Delivery appointments for DCs and dark stores are booked on each platform portal and tracked in a shared Excel tracker.

Missed appointments are escalated over email and phone.

## 3. Settlement and reconciliation

Settlement files are downloaded from each marketplace portal and reconciled manually in Excel against invoices.

Deductions that cannot be matched are raised as disputes through the seller portal; there is no visibility of leakage by reason code.

## 4. Content

PDP content is uploaded manually to each platform by the agency.
