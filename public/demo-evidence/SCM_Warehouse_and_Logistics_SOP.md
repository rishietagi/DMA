# Supply chain operations SOP — warehousing, logistics and planning — Illustrative

> Illustrative document created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Not a client document.

Owner: Supply Chain Excellence. Version 2.3, August 2026.

## 1. Demand and supply planning

The statistical forecast is generated in SAP IBP, but the sales team overrides it in Excel every month before the consensus meeting.

Production plans are created in SAP PP for each plant; capacity checks are still done manually in Excel.

## 2. Warehousing

Goods receipts at the warehouse dock are scanned into SAP using handhelds.

Cycle counts in the depots are recorded on paper and keyed into Excel at the end of each shift.

Stock across the 14 depots is checked manually every week; there is no live view by bin.

## 3. Transportation

Loads are planned by phone and email with transporters; there is no transport management system.

Transporters return proof of delivery on paper, and it reaches the team a week later.

Freight invoices are checked manually against rate cards in Excel.

## 4. Order fulfilment

Customer fill rate flows into a daily dashboard integrated with SAP, with standard reason codes.

## 5. Sustainability

Emissions and water data are collected once a year from plant spreadsheets and consolidated manually in Excel.
