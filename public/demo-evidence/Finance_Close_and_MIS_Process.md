# Finance close & MIS process note — Illustrative

> Illustrative document created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Not a Himalaya document.

## Close

The monthly close runs over nine working days.

Bank statements are uploaded to SAP daily and matched with standard rules; exceptions are listed in workpapers.

Intercompany open items build up as a backlog at quarter end.

Group consolidation runs in SAP BPC, integrated with the entity ledgers.

GST returns and e-invoices are generated from SAP DCS, integrated with GSTN.

## Management reporting

The MIS pack is assembled in Excel from SAP extracts and published as a Power BI report at entity level.

Marketing spend cannot be split by SKU, PG or channel in the MIS.

## Planning and expenses

Budgets are built in Excel templates emailed to business units, with actuals pulled from BW4HANA.

Travel and expense claims are submitted on paper or Excel and keyed into SAP by accounts payable.
