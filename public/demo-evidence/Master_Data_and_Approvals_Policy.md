# Master data & approvals policy — Illustrative

> Illustrative document created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Not a Himalaya document.

Supplier onboarding requires three approvals (procurement, finance and the MDM team), each requested by email.

Duplicate material codes are blocked by a rule-based check in the MDM workflow.

Artwork changes need regulatory, brand and QA sign-off; approvals are collected over email with PDF attachments.

Finance teams depend on IT for any new MIS report.
