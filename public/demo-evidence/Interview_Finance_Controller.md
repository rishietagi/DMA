# Interview notes — Finance Controller — Illustrative

> Illustrative notes created for the KPMG DMA proof of concept. Stakeholder role only; not a real interview.

Date: 23 Sep 2026. Interviewer: KPMG.

Intercompany reconciliation leaves a backlog every quarter and is the main reason close takes nine days.

The IBP plan is not the one finance uses: sales and supply chain keep parallel plan versions in Excel.

MIS at SKU and PG level is not possible today; marketing spend sits in a separate ledger.

Travel and expense claims arrive on paper and are keyed in by AP.
